# EEE 0713 1109: Introduction to Electrical Engineering

**Credit:** 3.00 Hours/Week  
**Institution:** Department of Computer Science & Engineering, Sylhet Engineering College (SEC)

---

## 📚 Syllabus Outline
1. **DC Circuit Analysis:** Ohm's Law, Kirchhoff's Current & Voltage Laws (KCL, KVL).
2. **Network Theorems:** Superposition, Thevenin's Theorem, Norton's Theorem, Maximum Power Transfer Theorem.
3. **Nodal & Mesh Analysis:** Standard and supernode/supermesh methods.
4. **Energy Storage Elements:** Inductors, capacitors, series/parallel combinations.
5. **Transient Response:** Natural and step responses of RL and RC circuits.
6. **AC Circuits:** Sinusoidal waveforms, phasors, impedance, apparent/real/reactive power, power factor correction.
