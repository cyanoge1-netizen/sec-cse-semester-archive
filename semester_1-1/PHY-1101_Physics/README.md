# PHY 1101: Physics

**Credit:** 3.00 Hours/Week  
**Institution:** Department of Computer Science & Engineering, Sylhet Engineering College (SEC)

---

## 📚 Syllabus Outline
1. **Wave Mechanics & Optics:** Wavefronts, Huygens' Principle, interference (Young's double slit), diffraction, polarization.
2. **Thermal Physics & Kinetic Theory:** Laws of thermodynamics, Maxwell's equipartition of energy, mean free path.
3. **Modern Physics:** Photoelectric effect, Compton scattering, de Broglie wavelength.
