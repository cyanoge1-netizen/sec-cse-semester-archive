# MATH 1101: Co-ordinate Geometry & Linear Algebra

**Credit:** 3.00 Hours/Week  
**Institution:** Department of Computer Science & Engineering, Sylhet Engineering College (SEC)

---

## 📚 Syllabus Outline
1. **Coordinate Geometry (2D & 3D):** Transformation of coordinates, pair of straight lines, general equation of second degree, standard conic sections.
2. **Matrices & Linear Systems:** Operations, matrix equality, determinants, matrix inversion ($A^{-1}$), Gaussian elimination, Row Echelon Form (REF).
3. **Rank & Nullity:** Definition as dimension of row/column space, rank-nullity theorem.
4. **Vector Spaces:** Subspaces, linear independence, basis, dimension.
5. **Eigenvalues & Eigenvectors:** Characteristic equation, Cayley-Hamilton theorem, diagonalization.
